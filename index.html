Furnished Property Rental Terms

These preliminary terms describe the basis on which an Ignite Living property may be reserved for a selected rental period.

1. Rental period
The property is made available for the specific move-in and move-out dates selected and confirmed for the reservation.

2. Occupancy
The property may only be occupied by the number of occupants declared during the reservation. Mayang Sari accommodates a maximum of 30 guests, with no extra charge per person.

3. Property use
The property is provided as a furnished private residential property for the agreed rental period. Occupants are expected to use the premises responsibly and comply with the applicable property rules.

4. Property condition
Occupants are responsible for maintaining the property in reasonable condition during the rental period and for reporting any material damage or problem promptly.

5. Payment
For Mayang Sari, the RM500 booking deposit is part of the total rental price. The remaining balance is payable on check-in day after the deposit has been paid. Cleaning is free. Cancellation and refund conditions will be provided before payment.

6. Confirmation
Submission of a rental request does not by itself constitute final confirmation. A reservation becomes confirmed once the applicable confirmation process has been completed.

7. Final terms
The final version of the applicable rental terms will be presented to the customer before a live reservation is completed.
